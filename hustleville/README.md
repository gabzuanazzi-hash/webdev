# Hustleville

Business & life age-up simulator. Open `index.html` in a browser (no build step).

## Loop
Tap **AGE** to pass a year. Income, business profit, investment returns, upkeep, random events and the death check all resolve on age-up.

👶 Born in a random country and family tier → 🧑 grow up → 💰 jobs / businesses / assets / investing → ❤️ date, marry, kids → 😈 crime and crazy stuff → 👑 fame, wealth, power → ⚰️ die and be reborn.

## Files
- `data.js` — countries, jobs, 10 niches x 3 business models (30 businesses), contract trap clauses, assets (homes, cars, clothes, watches, jets), crimes, activities
- `game.js` — state, age-up engine, panels, contract-signing desk
- `styles.css`, `index.html` — UI
- `assets/` — logo + wordmark (SVG)

## Contract signing
Starting or renewing a business opens the contract desk: negotiate revenue share, pick term and exclusivity, read (or skip) the fine print for hidden traps, then sign on the line. Unread traps cut profit and bite you later.

## Niches
Fashion, Tech & SaaS, Real Estate, Food & Beverage, Health & Fitness, Beauty, Gaming & Esports, Travel, Education, Events & Nightlife. Each has a Marketing Agency (online), Sales team (offline) and Product Sales (online) model.
